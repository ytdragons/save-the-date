The plan

Two days, and one place.

Thursday is the whole thing - ceremony, dinner, dancing - all at the vineyard, so once you are there you can put the keys away. Friday is lunch for anyone still standing.

  1. 13.05 Thursday

    The ceremony, then dinner

    Centennial Vineyards - 3:30pm to 11pm Arrive from 3. Everything happens in the one place: ceremony on the lawn at half three, drinks while the sun goes, dinner, then dancing until eleven. It gets cold quickly after dark - bring a coat you don't mind being seen in.

  2. 14.05 Friday

    Recovery lunch, if you're up to it

    Briars Inn - from 11am Completely casual. No speeches, no plan, no need to tell us you're coming. Turn up in whatever you woke up in, stay for one or stay for four.

Getting there

Ninety minutes from Sydney.

Everything below is what we would tell you on the phone, written down so nobody has to ask twice. Details firm up on the invitation.

Travel

Drive or take the train

Bowral sits off the Hume, about an hour and a half from the Sydney CBD without traffic.

  • Sydney → Bowral, 1h 30m by car
  • Central → Bowral, 2h on the Southern Highlands line
  • Parking on site; leave the car overnight if you'd rather
Rooms

Where to stay

Midweek in May is quiet, but book early anyway. We'll confirm held rates on the invitation.

  • Bowral - closest, walkable to breakfast
  • Berrima - fifteen minutes north
  • Moss Vale - fifteen minutes south
Practicals

What to expect

Highlands autumn: eight degrees in the morning, seventeen at lunch, and genuinely cold once the sun drops.

  • Ceremony is on grass - heels will sink
  • Bring a coat; the drinks hour is outside
  • Children welcome, gift details to follow
